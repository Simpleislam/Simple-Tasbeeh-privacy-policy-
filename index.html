Tasbeeh

Privacy policy · last updated 4 November 2026

Tasbeeh does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. Your count stays on your phone.

About this policy

This policy covers the Tasbeeh app for Android (package name com.simpleislam.tasbeeh), published by Simpleislam. It explains what the app does with information.

What the app stores on your phone

To remember where you left off, Tasbeeh saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • your current count
  • your settings: colour scheme, loop or strand view, bead sound on or off, and vibration on or off

This information never leaves your device. It is removed when you reset the count, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information of any kind.
  • It has no account, sign-in or registration.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request internet permission.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and no third-party code that collects data.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Permissions

Tasbeeh asks for one permission only: vibration, so that each bead can give a short pulse. Android grants this automatically and it can be switched off in the app's settings.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact
